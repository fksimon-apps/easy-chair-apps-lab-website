Features

Live Headroom

One hero number tells you how much more you can convert and stay inside your target bracket. Pick 12%, 22%, 24%, or 32% and the headline updates.

Marginal-Rate Curve

A chart of the tax on your next dollar against additional conversion. Bracket edges show as step-ups; SS-torpedo and OBBBA-phaseout regions show as bumps above the nominal rate.

Cliff Detection

IRMAA tiers (with 2-year forward perspective), LTCG 0%/15% threshold, OBBBA senior-bonus phaseout end — each with distance in dollars and annual cost if crossed.

Target / Paid / Left

A progress bar tracks your quarterly estimated payments against the safe-harbor target (lesser of 90% of this year or 100%/110% of last year). Federal and state, per quarter.

Pay-as-you-Convert

Enter a hypothetical conversion and see the additional federal tax to add to your next 1040-ES voucher. Or use the December-withholding suggestion to cure a whole-year shortfall at once.

Required Withdrawal (RMD)

IRS Uniform Lifetime Table lookup with the SECURE 2.0 age-73 / age-75 split. Flags the double-RMD trap if you deferred your first RMD past April 1.

State Tax, 50 + DC

Full math for 22 no-income-tax and flat-rate states. Progressive states get an info card with the top marginal rate and retirement-income treatment — honest, not approximated.

Printable PDF Report

Three pages modeled on a CPA tax-planning analysis: income summary, federal + state calculation, headroom analysis — with source citations on every constant.

JSON Export & Import

Export your household profile as a plain-JSON file to share with your tax preparer or move to a new device. Schema-versioned envelope, ready for next year's import.

Why TaxCalculator?

1

Built for the this-year horizon

Long-term planners answer “what’s my retirement plan?” TaxCalculator answers “what do I do this calendar year, and by when?”

2

Engines, not approximations

Cap-gains stacking, SS-torpedo, OBBBA phaseout, IRMAA cliffs, RMD basis — all modeled, not estimated.

3

Per-state treatment, honestly

Flat-tax states get the exact computation. Progressive states get the top rate and exemption flags with a “consult your preparer” note — not a misleading approximation.

4

No assumptions about your tax preparer

5

Completely on-device

No account, no analytics, no network calls. Your tax data never leaves your phone.

6
