Features

Tax-Aware Projections

Year-by-year projections covering federal income tax, capital gains, NIIT, Medicare IRMAA, ACA premium tax credits, and all 50 states plus D.C. Estate tax modeled at federal and state levels.

Social Security Optimizer

Evaluate claiming ages 62 to 70 for yourself and your spouse. Breakeven analysis, COLA projections, and the long-term dollar impact of each strategy.

Roth Conversion Engine

Three conversion modes: standard bracket fill, IRMAA-aware (size conversions to stay under Medicare surcharge thresholds), and minimize conversions. A unified Tax Payment Source setting — Auto, Taxable, or Traditional — controls where the tax on conversions and withdrawals is funded.

6-Tier Withdrawal Engine

Sources funds across six account tiers in priority order — Traditional, Investment, Annuity, Roth, Savings, and HSA — with five selectable strategies: Spending Needs, Fixed Amount, Percentage, Guardrails (Guyton-Klinger), and Tax-Aware.

RMD Engine

Annual Required Minimum Distributions calculated using the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table with SECURE Act 2.0 updates. RMD obligations are automatically factored into the withdrawal engine.

SEPP / 72(t) Calculator

Compute Substantially Equal Periodic Payments under all three IRS-approved methods: fixed amortization, fixed annuitization, and RMD method. Access retirement funds early without the 10% penalty.

ACA Subsidy Calculator

Estimates ACA marketplace premium tax credits and cost-sharing reductions based on projected MAGI and household size. Roth conversions and other income shifts show their ACA impact in real time.

Cost Basis & Tax-Loss Harvesting

Lot-level cost basis tracking for taxable accounts. Identifies tax-loss harvesting opportunities across holdings and integrates results into the projection engine.

Net Worth Tracker

Home equity, mortgage amortization, and total net worth projected alongside financial account balances. See how your complete balance sheet evolves through retirement.

Monte Carlo Risk Analysis

Historical simulation using 98 years of actual S&P 500 returns (1928–2025) plus configurable parametric simulation. Reports success rate, median ending balance, and sequence-of-returns risk.

Plan Permutation Grid Search

Grid search across withdrawal amounts, Social Security claiming ages, and Roth conversion strategies. Filter results by success rate and drill into any permutation’s full detailed report (Pro).

Scenario Comparison & Sensitivity

Side-by-side comparison of two plans. Tornado-chart sensitivity analysis shows which assumptions — return, inflation, withdrawal, Social Security timing — most affect your outcome.

Built to Teach, Not Just Calculate

Retirement finance is full of jargon and non-obvious tax mechanics. Retirement Planner explains them in plain language, right where the numbers appear, so you learn the “why” behind every result — a strong fit for DIY planners, anyone preparing for a meeting with a financial advisor, and students of personal finance or tax.

Built-in Glossary (100+ terms)

A plain-language definition for every piece of jargon — MAGI, IRMAA, NIIT, RMD, cost basis, qualified dividends, provisional income, sequence-of-returns risk, and more — reachable from info buttons throughout the app.

Guided Tutorials on Every Screen

Each report page and editor has its own walkthrough: what the page shows, how to read each column, tips for acting on it, and warnings about common mistakes — plus conceptual overviews of hard topics like how MAGI drives IRMAA and NIIT.

Tables That Show Their Work

Instead of a single mystery number, reports break results into their parts. The Capital Gains Detail table shows Investment Sold next to Realized Gain so you can see the cost-basis math; the MAGI Detail table lists every income component that sums to MAGI.

Reference Data Browser

View the actual IRS tax brackets, capital-gains rates, IRMAA thresholds, RMD Uniform Lifetime Table, Social Security adjustment factors, and state tax rates — the real source numbers behind the projections, not a black box.

Clear, Stated Assumptions

Modeling choices are spelled out — all dividends treated as qualified, a default 50% cost basis you can override per account, standard deduction only — so you understand exactly what the projection does and does not model.

For Young Investors

Although the app targets retirement, its projection engine models decades of compounding and taxes — which makes it a surprisingly good tool for younger savers who want to understand how today’s choices play out over a lifetime. It answers the question most budgeting apps never touch: why does it matter where I save, and what will it cost me later?

See How Money Grows

Model contributions plus market growth across 30–40 years and watch a modest monthly amount compound into a retirement number — with historical S&P 500 return data (1928–2025) and Monte Carlo analysis showing the range of outcomes, not just a single line.

Understand the Tax Trade-offs Early

Compare saving in a Traditional account (deduct now, taxed later — including RMDs and possible IRMAA surcharges) versus a Roth (pay tax now, grow and withdraw tax-free) and see the difference decades out, in real dollars.

Learn What Each Account Costs You Later

The app makes the downstream tax picture concrete: Traditional means ordinary income plus required withdrawals; Roth is tax-free; a taxable brokerage brings capital gains, qualified dividends, and cost-basis mechanics.

The Case for Tax Diversification

By modeling Traditional, Roth, and taxable buckets together, a young saver can see why having all three — and starting a taxable or Roth balance early — gives far more flexibility to control taxes in retirement.

Contribution Rules Built In

SECURE Act 2.0 contribution and catch-up limits are modeled, so the “how much can I put in” question is answered as you plan.
